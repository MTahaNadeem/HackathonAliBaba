# Three-minute demo

0:00–0:25 — Problem: A student sees many degrees, disconnected courses and unclear job requirements. CourseCompass AI connects those decisions in one personal path.

0:25–1:05 — Open Profile. Choose A-levels, interests Research/Numbers/Society, Mathematics/Economics, Islamabad, a semester budget and weekly study time. Explain that foreign grades need official equivalence. Save preferences. Explore Economics and compare it with another path. Open the trial activity, then choose a direction.

1:05–1:40 — Open Universities. Show a current sourced NUST fee, the budget comparison and missing-information state. Open an official source. Explain that CourseCompass AI distinguishes unknown admission rules from eligibility and does not predict acceptance.

1:40–2:25 — Open Jobs & skills, select Economic research assistant (clearly an example), analyse it and explain self-reported versus not-yet-demonstrated skills. Create the plan. Open a resource, show its practice task, mark one step complete, and download the plan.

2:25–2:50 — Open Ask CourseCompass AI and ask about the chosen path. Enable Gemini only when configured and permitted; identify guided mode honestly if the API is unavailable.

2:50–3:00 — Architecture: PostgreSQL from day one, accounts with RLS, source-backed rules and free services. Expand verified coverage and partnerships on the same database.

Before presenting: verify the exact deployed audience access, sign in and confirm cloud saves if Supabase is configured, test the selected Gemini key without billing, and keep a downloadable plan ready. Never call this a live job feed, a guaranteed career diagnosis, or a verified production launch.
